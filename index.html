Campfire

Campfire is Hyde’s platform for training, fine-tuning, and deploying your own specialist models.

hyde’s platform

Your Cloud or Hyde's Cloud: You Choose

1.1

Campfire ships as a CLI-tool that plugs into your existing cloud infrastructure and works with all major data-lake platforms.

1.2

For teams that want to forget about DevOps, Campfire can handle infrastructure provisioning across dedicated reserved or shared on-demand GPU capacity.

Automate The Full Training Arc

2.1

Campfire lets you build your evaluation environments from your own tasks to allow models to reflect what accuracy means for your company.

2.2

Campfire lets you automate the full arc of model training and deployment through a guided dashboard or a single command.

Eval-Based Deployment

3.1

Campfire lets you evaluate and track every single training checkpoint run against both public and custom benchmarks.

3.2

Campfire ships with built in support for A/B testing and easy rollbacks, enabling you to deploy models with confidence.

Continual Learning Made Possible

4.1

Campfire excels at ingesting data from production deployments to improve benchmarks and re-train models.

4.2

Your model becomes a continually improving asset, enabling you to widen the gap between your specialist models and off-the-shelf APIs.
